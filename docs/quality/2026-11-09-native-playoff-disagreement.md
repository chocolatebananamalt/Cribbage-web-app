## Scope

The dedicated proposal adds three immutable private tables (reopen headers, attempt evidence, own-operation closures), five revoked PUBLIC wrappers and two literal-false gates, and replaces seven private Playoff bodies. It depends on pinned existing private Playoff/own/latest-source proposals; it is not an installed migration or a standalone hosted update. A fresh empty attempt digest includes its immutable generation ID.

Valid conflicting DD/DP/PP records remain incomplete with no winner. Explicit paper disagreements require a full independent re-entry that actually differs. Metadata-only paper-first own reads expose a review block without the hidden evidence. The own player cannot confirm or advance a disputed game. A closed disagreement gate returns a scoped exact-retry result after all authorization/source/history checks, without committing a new record.

Director requests bind project, actor/session, event, context, slot, game, current source/match/history/policy digests and a required reason. Only the current verified nonself Director can act. Missing policy does not become an invented zero-approval default. Separate-approval, already completed, later-game/dependent-result and downstream Finance/pool-final cases remain blocked. The Director chooses no winner. Reopening creates an empty attempt for the same players/format/game; fresh independent evidence is still required.

Normal-auth transport, retained CAS controller, reason-required Director UI/provider and a literal-false route are supplied separately from the qualifying flow. The route is inaccessible and intentionally unadvertised; a future enablement requires actual Director navigation and provider acceptance. No hosted role grant, feature activation or publication occurred.

## Own-operation recovery

Legacy `evidence_mismatch` holds retain their verbatim original prepared request. A dedicated read-only lookup may prove an exact accepted record, a strictly older attempt superseded by an audited reopening, a previously closed operation, or the still-current/uncommitted original digital submission. Only a known local mismatch hold plus the last proof allows an explicit reviewed retry of the SAME operation/body. The hold is not removed before dispatch; no automatic retry, rebasing or replacement nonce is introduced.

An explicit owner action can close a positively proven absent/obsolete operation, reserving its original nonce with an immutable receipt and audit. Still-current, unknown, corrupt, foreign and unavailable histories cannot be cleared. Accepted/superseded/closed recovery archives the original raw data and authoritative proof atomically before clearing the local pending slot. Unknown outcome, storage failure, CAS race or changed identity preserves pending work. A closure supplies no current state; the host refreshes the own read before new entry.

Rolling back to older Playoff bodies after new attempts exist can invalidate recovery/result proof; a future rollback should disable new writes while preserving compatible readers and immutable history, rather than blindly replace functions or delete evidence. A hosted release requires a separately reviewed dependency/permission package, recovery plan and authorization. Real-device and paper-role acceptance remain incomplete. No new owner payout, seeding, membership or correction policy was inferred.

Later/dependent/completed corrections and separate approval remain unsupported. Only two DD generations are proved; unbounded repetition and real-provider timeout behavior are not accepted. A later narrow optimization should validate each immutable ancestry header/row once, without trusting a client or session cache. It was deliberately not mixed into the settled correctness proof.
