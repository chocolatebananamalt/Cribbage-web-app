# Native rehearsal disputed-game reopening — private checkpoint

Scope: an audited, nonself Director can reopen an accepted, persisted conflicting qualifying-game bundle for fresh verification. No winner or replacement score is chosen by the Director. Original entries and their receipts remain immutable. This is simulated rehearsal evidence, not ordinary score correction, official ACC adjudication, membership determination or disbursement.

## Acceptance and boundaries

- Exact project, actor/session, tournament, event, Start, game, source and history bindings; stale, concurrent, foreign and revoked requests fail safely.
- Existing saved correction policy must have zero additional approvals. A policy requiring another approval is blocked rather than downgraded. Required Director reason is retained.
- New attempt begins empty and retains the same players, Tournament IDs and qualifying game. Digital/digital, paper/digital and paper/paper records must pass the original independent submission/review and confirmation rules again.
- Historical receipts can close their exact older pending request only with server-proven supersession. An old receipt alone cannot verify current evidence or authorize a new write. Unknown outcomes remain pending and never auto-rebase or resend.
- Completed games, later affected-player evidence, downstream closure and self-participating Director are blocked. No cascade into qualification, playoffs, final awards, pools or official results.

## Remaining limits

This deliberately covers persisted conflicting bundles. A lone digital typo, or internally consistent but mistyped paper capture whose independent reviewer disagrees before any rejection is persisted, is not covered. That requires a separate auditable discrepancy admission, not deleting entries or copying an incorrect score. A separate-approval policy workflow, Director's own match, late dependencies and ordinary official result corrections remain unsupported.

All three RPCs, SQL table/function access, route/page/provider gates remain default-off; this source is not an installed migration. The page is unadvertised until provider acceptance and navigation are supplied. It is outside the immutable 216-file public release candidate. No hosted change, access activation, publication retry, photo transfer, membership rule or money movement was performed.

The required chained `pnpm verify` cannot be claimed: pnpm is unavailable in this shell and the previously denied dependency audit was not retried. Executed offline equivalents and any remaining gaps are recorded in the checkpoint evidence; there is no production or real-device readiness claim.
