The fresh CSV repeats this same reader under current cookies instead of trusting a browser snapshot. Source/Start/context/result pins and simulation/no-award/no-acceptance labels survive export; private actors, session IDs, profiles and raw review evidence are not exported. It adds no larger-field bracket generation or result-reader approval, Consolation/Satellite/team mapping, actual membership lookup, official ACC reconciliation or report transmission. All proposed points, including nonmember hypotheticals, remain distinct from actual awards and ACC acceptance. Missing official event-player records remain pending, never rejected.
