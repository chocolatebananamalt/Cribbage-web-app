# Four-co-director tournament role lifecycle

Tournament officials are one primary director plus up to four co-directors. The
primary director remains singular and is not transferable in this workflow.
Exact-email invitations are short-lived (seven days), single-use, and consume a
slot while pending. Acceptance requires the invited person to sign in with the
same app email and explicitly accept. The server records every lifecycle
transition through idempotent receipts and immutable audit events. Only the
primary director manages ordinary invitations and role status; a platform
administrator can use a reasoned, audited emergency revoke/restore override.
