# 2026-09-07 — Score-entry language

## Decision

The score-entry panel uses plain player-facing language:

- its heading is `Record game result`;
- its numeric field is `Spread Points`, with range validation retained but no visible `1–121` cue;
- it does not show prototype workflow labels such as `FAST ENTRY`, `1 of 3`, or `Derived result`;
- informal player feedback may say `Skunk`, `Double Skunk`, or `Triple Skunk` and show the corresponding icon without a disclaimer.

## Boundary

The friendly skunk labels/icons remain presentation only. Official game points stay derived as 0/2/3, and the informal bands do not enter standings, records, or ACC exports.

User-approved prototype review, 2026-09-07.
