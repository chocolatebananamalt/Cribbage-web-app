## Actual ledger read bridge

The normalized ledger projection distinguishes documented actual from simulation,
requires an explicit complete event registry and global pool ownership, and
partitions each unique physical source exactly across event/fund allocations.
Duplicate physical evidence, foreign events/pools, void allocations and imbalance
fail closed. Restricted/unreviewed classifications and negative funds prevent
award calculation. Optional Q/Side money is never added to regular funds.

The legacy adapter validates existing receipt and expense workspaces and references
their current immutable source-event IDs, not fee times headcount or paid-in-full
claims. All histories are checked for cross-owner/cross-kind event-ID aliases before
selecting active heads. Missing or obsolete allocation bindings block. A voided
receipt cannot remain funding evidence. Private names are not emitted by the totals.

These are pure source adapters: no RPC, actual receipt writing, ledger finalization
or money movement is implied. The existing PaymentWorkspace has no tournament ID;
its server fetch must be scoped to the same tournament, under a consistent source
reader. Client-posted facts, review booleans and digests are not proof of receipts,
classification approval, ACC benefit status or pool eligibility.
